Finding the campus

Look for the petrol pump.

Narshi Fatha is the easiest landmark. The campus is at Gat No 63, beside Jai Sai Petrol Pump, past Devda Nagar. If a map app struggles with the full address, search for the school by name or use the plus code P46W+H5.

For a first visit, allow a little extra time. The office can guide you in on the phone if the turn is easy to miss.
