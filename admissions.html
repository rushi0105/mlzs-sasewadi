When you apply

What to bring to the office.

Documents are usually requested at the application stage. The office will confirm the exact list for your child's class.

  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school report and transfer certificate, where applicable
  • Address proof and a government photo identity
  • Passport-size photographs of the child and parents
  • Immunisation record, for early years

Questions

Before you ask, it may be here.

Which classes are open for 2026-27?

Enquiries are open from Nursery to Grade 10. Seat availability changes through the year, so please confirm the position for your child's class with the office before you apply.

Is there an entrance test?

The process includes an interaction with the child and family. Ask the office what it involves for your child's class. It is a conversation about readiness, not a competition.

What is the age criterion for admission?

Age criteria follow the applicable state and CBSE norms for each class. The office will confirm the cut-off date for the year you are applying in.

Do you provide school transport?

Please ask the office. Routes and availability change by year and by area, and we would rather confirm your address than make a promise the bus cannot keep.

What are the school timings?

The office opens at 7:00 am. Class timings are confirmed at the time of admission and can differ slightly by stage.

Can we visit before deciding?

Yes, and we would prefer it. A campus visit on a working day tells you more than any conversation on the phone. Call or send an enquiry and we will arrange a time.

How do we find out about fees?

Ask the school office for the current fee schedule in writing, including any one-time charges. Fees are not published on this website because they change by class and year.

Still deciding?

Start with a phone call.

No form, no commitment. Tell us your child's age and class, and we will tell you honestly whether this is the right fit.
