01 · The school

Good teaching is quiet, consistent and personal.

Children learn best when someone knows them well. That is the simple idea behind everything here: small enough to notice a child, serious enough to prepare one.

Classrooms run on routine and conversation. Reading happens every day. Questions are welcome. Mistakes are treated as information, not failure. Behind the classroom, teachers plan together and review what is actually working.

  • Foundations

    Reading, writing and number sense, taught properly and practised often.

  • Attention

    Teachers who know each child's strengths, habits and next step.

  • Room to grow

    Sport, art, music and play held inside the curriculum, not bolted on.
