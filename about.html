A school with a point of view.

Mount Litera Zee School, Sasewadi is a CBSE school in Hingoli with a clear brief: teach well, know every child, and let childhood do its work.

Our story

Built for the children of Hingoli.

The school was opened to give families in and around Hingoli a serious CBSE option close to home. The idea was not to build the biggest school. It was to build one where a child is known by name, where teaching is planned rather than improvised, and where an ordinary school day is worth turning up for.

The campus sits at Narshi Fatha, near Jai Sai Petrol Pump, within easy reach of Devda Nagar and Siddharth Nagar. It is organised around a simple rhythm: read, think, practise, play, reflect. The building supports that rhythm. It does not compete with it.

We are part of the Mount Litera Zee School network and keep the best of that system: a written curriculum, ongoing teacher development and a clear view of what children should know at each stage. The rest is local. The teachers, the families, the language in the corridor, the particular children in front of us.

What we believe

Four ideas we are willing to be judged by.

Values are easy to print on a wall. These are the ones we put into the timetable, the report and the conversation with a parent.

Childhood is not a race.

Children develop at different speeds. We teach the child in front of us, not an average of the class.

Reading is the foundation.

A child who reads well can learn almost anything. We protect time for reading every single day.

Teachers matter most.

No building, screen or slogan replaces a well-prepared teacher who knows the subject and the child.

Character is taught, not announced.

Punctuality, honesty and care are practised in small daily ways, not saved for a special assembly.

How we teach

Six things every child should leave with.

Not a list of subjects. A list of capabilities. The syllabus serves these, not the other way around.

Language and literacy

Reading, writing, listening and speaking, taught explicitly and practised across the day.

Numeracy and reasoning

Number sense before shortcuts. The confidence to explain how an answer was reached.

Inquiry and environment

Questions worth chasing, and the habit of looking closely at the world outside the window.

Arts and expression

Music, art and performance as serious ways of thinking, not decoration for the annual day.

Sport and wellbeing

Daily movement, outdoor play and the ordinary lessons of winning, losing and trying again.

Community and service

An understanding that a school is a shared place, and that contribution is part of learning.

Ask us anything

Come and form your own view.

The best way to understand a school is to stand in it on a normal working day. We would rather answer a hard question than write a longer brochure.
